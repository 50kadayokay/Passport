## Round 6 — security headers in vercel.json (S4 assignment, 2026-11-01)

Two rules, added after the existing Cache-Control ones:
- `/(.*)`: `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` on everything.
- `/((?!api/|assets/).*)` (every page): `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`,
  `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'self' capacitor://localhost` and `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`.
  Browsers that understand frame-ancestors ignore XFO; older ones get SAMEORIGIN.

**What frames or embeds us, and what was exempted:**

| Route | Why | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Iframes in `src/` and `public/` (marketing phones `/app?embed=1` and `/appdemo`; admin, ProfileEditor, MissionControl and ConferenceStudio previews; booth template `/confv3demo` and `/conference`; study pages) | All are same-origin paths: relative, or built from `window.location.origin` | `'self'` covers them; no exemption |
| Booth phone profile (`ConferenceScenes.jsx`) | Falls back to the app host when its origin isn't http(s), i.e. inside a native webview | `capacitor://localhost` allowed as an ancestor |
| Investor app (Capacitor) | Loads its own `dist` bundle top-level (no `server.url`); Vercel headers never reach it | nothing needed |
| Conference iPad app (Passport-ConfApp) | Bundles ConferenceV3, which has no iframes | nothing needed |
| `/api/*`: site-content and site-manifest (company websites, cross-origin fetch), og-news (images for crawlers), conference-config, messages, stripe-webhook | Fetched, never framed | nosniff only; no framing or referrer headers |
| `/assets/*` | Scripts and styles | nosniff only; immutable cache kept |
| `/n/:id` share page | Top-level page for people and crawlers | gets page headers (harmless) |
| Stripe checkout, portal and OAuth returns | Top-level navigations; the referrer sends the origin only | nothing needed |

HSTS is left to Vercel's default (the smoke still checks it). No `includeSubDomains`, so other mineex.ca subdomains are not affected.

## Round 16 — 0093: a database rate limit for direct listing reports (S4, user-approved Option B, 2026-11-01)

**Round 16b, 0093 redesigned on S4's gateway measurements** (staging, 2026-11-01). The user's rule: never trust a client-controlled header.
- **Measurements.** cf-connecting-ip is always set by the gateway, and a client copy is rejected (403). x-forwarded-for keeps client entries and appends the real address LAST. x-real-ip is stripped. true-client-ip passes through (forgeable).
- **Client.** `listing_report_client_ip()` uses cf-connecting-ip, else the LAST XFF hop; it never reads x-real-ip or true-client-ip, and trims all whitespace. With no address (a direct session), one 'unknown' bucket at the per-client limit, with no network counter.
- **Network.** `listing_report_network()`: the /24 (IPv4) or /48 (IPv6) of the address; anything that isn't an address has no network. Limit: 20 per hour.
- **Global.** Raised to 600 per hour. Lock order: global, then network, then client.
- **Keys.** Stored via `listing_report_key()`: 'lr:' / 'ln:' + 32 hex of SHA-256(salt | value).
- **Residual risk** (documented in the migration): filling the global ceiling needs 30+ distinct networks within one hour, and then refuses reports only until the hour rolls over. Alert on the global PT429.
- **Pruning.** Every limited insert deletes up to 50 of OUR counters older than a day (`for update skip locked`), so the global rows stay at about 25 (one an hour) and nothing grows without bound. /api/submit's counters are left alone.
- **1.3.x unchanged:** one object, return=minimal; refusals are non-2xx.
