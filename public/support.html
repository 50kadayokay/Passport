Support

Contact us

Common questions

  • Reset your password: on the sign-in screen, choose "Forgot password?" and follow the emailed link.
  • Delete your account: go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile and your messages from the live service. See our Privacy Policy for what deletion does and does not cover.
  • News and company information: MineEx brings together public company press releases and industry sources, with attribution and links to the originals, alongside AI-assisted summaries. Summaries can contain errors and are not a substitute for a company's original disclosure. MineEx provides information and is not investment advice.

For companies

If you represent a mining company and want to know about a MineEx presence, email support@mineex.ca or use the contact form. Paid services for companies are arranged directly and governed by a separate written agreement.
