Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · MineEx is operated by Liquid Jungle Management Ltd.

This policy explains what personal information we collect through the MineEx investor application and the MineEx website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. In this policy, "MineEx" means the MineEx application and website; "we", "us" and "our" mean Liquid Jungle Management Ltd., the company that operates MineEx.

Questions, requests or complaints: support@mineex.ca.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and a password. Authentication is handled by our authentication provider; your password is stored by that provider in hashed form and is not visible to us in plain text. You may optionally add profile details such as a display name, location, investor type and areas of interest.
  • Messages to companies. If you message a company through MineEx, we store the message and the conversation so it appears in your inbox, and we deliver it to that company by email so they can reply. The company receives the content of your message and the information needed to respond to you.
  • Preferences and saved items. Your Following, Favourites, Watchlist and saved or liked items are stored on your device, so they work whether or not you are signed in. When you are signed in, they are also saved to your MineEx account so they are available when you sign in again or on another device. Some actions — such as following a company from a news card — are saved only to your account and require you to be signed in.
  • Contact and sales inquiries. If you submit a form on our website we collect what you provide — typically your name, email address, company, ticker symbol and message, plus anything else you choose to include — so we can respond and correspond with you about MineEx products and services.
  • Company Portal content. If your company uses the MineEx Company Portal, we store the profile information, press releases, images and other materials submitted through it, together with the account details of the people who use it on your company's behalf.
  • Technical information. Standard information generated when software runs — such as app version, device type, error and diagnostic logs, and the request metadata our hosting and database providers record in the ordinary course of serving a request, which can include IP address. We use this to keep MineEx working and secure. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not operate an advertising network.
  • Push notifications. In the mobile applications, if you allow notifications, we store the device token issued by Apple (iOS) or Google (Android) together with your notification preferences so we can send the alerts you asked for. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

How we use information

  • To operate MineEx, sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • To show company profiles, news and other content, and to keep your preferences and saved items available to you.
  • To deliver messages between you and companies, and to let them reply.
  • To respond to contact and sales inquiries and correspond with you about MineEx.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To provide AI-assisted features you ask for, such as company summaries and structured versions of public company information (see AI processing below).
  • To keep MineEx reliable — debugging, diagnostics and service monitoring.
  • To protect MineEx, our users and third parties against fraud, abuse, security incidents and misuse of the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to enforce our Terms of Use.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for behavioural or targeted advertising, and we do not share it with third-party advertising networks.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run MineEx. They are independent companies, not owned or controlled by us, and they receive personal information only as reasonably necessary to perform services for us, subject to the terms that apply between us and them.

  • Supabase — database, file storage and authentication for accounts, profiles, messages and company content.
  • Vercel — hosting and delivery of the MineEx website, application and API.
  • Postmark — transactional email, including delivering your messages to companies, carrying their replies back to you, and sending us website inquiries.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic — AI services used for the features described below.
  • Stripe — payment processing for company subscriptions. Card details are handled by Stripe; we do not receive or store full payment card numbers.
  • Apple and Google — delivery of push notifications to iOS and Android devices respectively (Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging), where you have enabled them.

AI processing

MineEx uses third-party AI services to produce company summaries and to structure public company information such as press releases and website content into a consistent format.

What is sent to those services is company and news material — public disclosure, press releases and company-supplied profile content — not your account details. Private messages between investors and companies are not sent to any AI provider.

AI-generated summaries can contain errors or omit information. They are not a substitute for a company's original disclosure. See our Terms of Use.

Processing outside Canada

We are based in British Columbia, Canada. Some of the providers above process or store information outside Canada, including in the United States. Information processed in another country may be subject to the laws of that country, including lawful access requests by courts, law enforcement and government authorities there.

Consent and your choices

When you provide information for a particular purpose — creating an account, sending a message, submitting an inquiry — you authorize us to use it for that purpose, as described in this policy and as permitted by applicable law.

You can withdraw your consent, subject to legal and contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide a feature or service that depends on the information — for example, we cannot deliver a message to a company without sending it to them.

Submitting a contact or sales inquiry lets us respond to that inquiry and correspond with you about MineEx products and services. It does not, on its own, sign you up for unrelated marketing campaigns.

Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy — to provide the service, meet our legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security and enforce our agreements.

You can delete your account from the app (Profile → Settings → Delete account), or by following our account deletion instructions, which removes your profile and your messages from the live service. Deleting your account may not immediately remove information from routine system backups, and it does not remove information we are required or permitted by law to retain. Where a company has already received your message by email, that copy is in their hands and is governed by their own practices.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. Access to user data in our database is restricted by per-user security rules.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Access, correction and deletion

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, ask questions about this policy, or make a privacy complaint. Contact support@mineex.ca. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.
