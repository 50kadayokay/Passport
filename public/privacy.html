Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

MineEx (the "App") is operated by Liquid Jungle Management Ltd. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions? Contact us at nitrospicexxx@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details. When you create an account we collect your email address and a securely hashed password (handled by our authentication provider). You may optionally provide profile details.
  • Messages. If you message a company, we store the message content and the conversation so it appears in your inbox, and we deliver it to that company by email so they can respond.
  • On-device preferences. Your Following, Favourites and Watchlist are stored locally on your device, not on our servers.
  • Basic technical data. Standard information such as app version and error logs needed to keep the App working. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

  • To provide the App — sign-in, showing company profiles, and delivering your messages to companies.
  • To keep the App secure and reliable, and to respond to support requests.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We share limited data with providers that run the App on our behalf, only as needed:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (account and message storage).
  • Postmark — email delivery for the message bridge between investors and companies.
  • Vercel — application hosting.
  • Anthropic — generates the AI company summaries from public information.

These providers process data under their own security and privacy commitments.

Your messages and companies

Company profiles and public disclosures shown in the App come from public sources. When you send a company a message, the recipient (the company's contact) can see your message and the email address or name you send it under, so they can reply.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and messages until you delete your account. You can permanently delete your account and its associated data at any time from Profile → Delete account inside the App, or by following our account deletion instructions. On-device lists are removed when you delete the App.

Children

MineEx is intended for adults making their own investment research decisions and is not directed to children under 17.
